# Insurance Management Platform

A full-stack Insurance Management Platform built with the MERN ecosystem (React + Node.js) and PostgreSQL. The application digitizes insurance operations by allowing administrators, insurance agents, and customers to manage policies, claims, premium payments, documents, and reports through a centralized system.

---

## 🚀 Features

### Authentication & Authorization
- JWT Authentication
- Role-Based Access Control (Admin, Agent, Customer)
- Password Encryption using bcrypt

### Customer Management
- Customer Registration
- Customer Profile
- Update Customer Information
- Search Customers
- Customer History

### Policy Management
- Create Insurance Policies
- View Active Policies
- Renew Policies
- Cancel Policies
- Policy Expiry Tracking

### Claim Management
- Submit Claims
- Upload Supporting Documents
- Claim Verification
- Claim Approval / Rejection
- Claim History

### Premium Tracking
- Premium Payments
- Payment History
- Due Date Tracking
- Overdue Alerts

### Document Management
- Upload Documents
- Download Documents
- View Uploaded Files

### Reports & Dashboard
- Active Policies
- Expired Policies
- Premium Collection
- Customer Growth
- Claim Statistics
- Monthly Reports
